# Logo AMIRA — revisi simbol A

Dibuat pada 13 Oktober 2026 menggunakan built-in ImageGen. Logo menggunakan monogram A membulat dengan bentuk yang mengacu pada halaman buku dan aksen lipatan kuning. Tulisan lowercase `amira` mempertahankan karakter ramah dari versi sebelumnya.

## File dan penggunaan

- `amira-logo-primary-v2.png` — logo horizontal dengan simbol dan tulisan, 2172 × 724 px. Untuk header website, slide, dokumen, dan materi promosi berlatar terang.
- `amira-symbol-v2.png` — simbol A tanpa tulisan, 1254 × 1254 px. Untuk ikon dan avatar.


Kedua logo berupa PNG raster beresolusi tinggi dengan alpha transparan. Gunakan proporsi asli, beri ruang kosong di sekeliling logo, dan jangan meregangkan gambar. Warna acuan: navy `#243E78`, cobalt `#3157D5`, golden yellow `#FFCB49`.

Aset ada di `public/assets/brand/`. Salinan dengan nama `amira-logo-primary.png` dan `amira-symbol.png` juga telah diperbarui ke revisi ini. Paket ZIP dan versi lama disimpan sebagai artefak lokal dan tidak menjadi bagian dari source repository.

## Prompt — simbol A

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: standalone AMIRA educational AI logo symbol, usable as a small app icon. Design ONE distinct flat vector-style symbol on a genuinely transparent background. Primary request: a friendly premium rounded geometric uppercase A monogram whose two bold legs subtly read as the pages of an open book. The education meaning comes from the A's own geometry, not from a separate book placed inside it. Use two broad continuous balanced page-like shapes meeting near a softly rounded top, an unmistakable A silhouette, a generous clear central triangular counter and a short bold crossbar. Main fill solid cobalt #3157D5. A single integrated golden-yellow #FFCB49 page-edge/tip accent may occupy only 10-15 percent of the mark. Keep it very simple, crisp and recognizable at 24 pixels, with robust rounded edges and generous gaps; must remain an A when viewed in one color. Compatible with the friendly lowercase rounded 'amira' wordmark. Center on a square transparent canvas with about 10 percent padding. No wordmark or text except the abstract A symbol. No moon, crescent, lunar motifs, stars, sparkle, orbit, circle frame, speech bubble, chat tail, robot face, graduation cap, brain, separate arrow, thin outline, tiny page lines, decorative folds, gradients, light reflections, shadow, glow, texture or 3D. Avoid resemblance to a heart, butterfly, ribbon or a generic corporate triangle. Render a simple fresh educational brand mark, not a presentation/mockup, no background or checkerboard.
