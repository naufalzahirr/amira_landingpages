## Source discrepancy: identity in Ministry reporting

The [Ministry press-release webpage](https://www.kemendikdasmen.go.id/siaran-pers/14267-inovasi-kecerdasan-buatan-dan-konservasi-lingkungan-antarkan-tiga-guru-smk-raih-apresiasi-gtk-2025) and [official PDF, press release 841/sipers/A6/XI/2025](https://internal-portal.kemdikbud.go.id/download/file/1531), dated 28 November 2025, attribute AMIRA to “Naufal Faadhilah, SMKN 1 Katapang, Bandung.” This conflicts with the user's manuscripts, the school/Kepri award reporting, and the June 2026 official report naming AMIRA at SMKN 4 Tanjungpinang.

The precise cause is not established. Do not silently correct or quote the conflicting identity, and do not use this release as the landing page's main award/product source. Secondary Melintas reports repeat that attribution and add no independent verification:

- [Melintas — three transformative vocational teachers, December 2025](https://www.melintas.id/pendidikan/346899784/inilah-3-guru-smk-yang-berhasil-meraih-juara-apresiasi-guru-dan-tenaga-kependidikan-kategori-transformatif-tahun-2025)
- [Melintas — AI innovation, 1 December 2025](https://www.melintas.id/pendidikan/amp/346899275/luar-biasa-inovasi-kecerdasan-buatan-bawa-guru-smk-menang-gtk-2025)

The November 2025 Kepri article also names the former Ministry of Education, Culture, Research and Technology as organizer, whereas the contemporaneous Ministry release is from the Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education. Use “Indonesia's 2025 GTK Appreciation Awards” in translated copy instead of copying the stale organizer wording.

## Product-claim boundaries

The existing project documentation records system-prompt, subject, and PDF-resource configuration in the supplied 2025 materials; prompt presets and a learning log are discussed in the manuscript. The current landing-page configuration and chat remain illustrative prepared examples.

Keep the following distinct in English copy:

- **Documented classroom work:** the educational AI companion and its guidance-first learning approach, supported by the supplied references. Describe as classroom work/prototype history, without unverified outcome percentages.
- **Product concept:** educators configure mentor behavior, instructions, and reference materials; learners interact with the mentor. Show the configuration journey as an illustrative concept where implementation has not been verified.
- **Future direction:** separate mentors, class-access controls, and a scalable teacher/student dashboard where these are not confirmed in the product audit.

The public sources above do not verify additional implemented features. The 64-versus-36 sample-size discrepancy already identified in the supplied documents remains unresolved. Do not add numerical learning-impact claims from either the manuscripts or the unrelated ITS research.

On 13 October 2026, the developer clarified in this conversation that AMIRA had a previously working app and is now being rebuilt. The landing page records this as first-party product status, rather than implying there has never been an app or that the new app is publicly available.

The developer also clarified that sharing AMIRA with educators began in 2025. The journey milestone is therefore labeled “Since 2025”; the independently reported 2026 forum remains a later sharing activity. The report link explicitly identifies the 2026 coverage and is not presented as evidence for the start date.
